Five little reasons to stay up together.

Love Arcade is a free, browser-based multiplayer game made for two people in a relationship. Whether you are on different sides of a city or in different time zones, you can turn an ordinary evening into a virtual date. There is no account to create and nothing to install: one person creates a room and shares the invite link with their partner. Both of you open it in a modern browser on your phone, tablet, or computer. Developed by azhardotcoder, the project combines simple romantic games with optional chat and voice calling.

Try Truth or Dare for a sweet challenge where the second player approves each completed task before the turn changes. Play Love Quiz to discover your shared memories and favorite moments. Would You Rather? helps you choose between dream dates, getaways, and little surprises. Love Notes is a place to write something thoughtful and reveal both letters together. Heart Match is a cooperative memory-card game where partners take turns. Every activity is designed for shared play rather than a single-player quiz.

Common questions

How do we play together from different places?

Open Love Arcade on both phones. One player chooses Create our room and copies the invitation. Send it directly to your partner, who can join through the link or enter its 12-character room code. Keep both tabs open while you play. The host's browser coordinates the rounds, turns, and shared game state.

Do we have to download an app or create an account?

No. Love Arcade is designed to work inside modern browsers with JavaScript enabled. A username is simply a nickname for your current session. For the best experience, use an updated version of Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox and a reliable connection.

Can we talk during the games?

Yes. Use the chat button to exchange messages while in the room, or start an optional voice call. The second player can accept or decline a call. Both browsers must allow microphone access for voice chat. The mute and sound buttons control your own microphone and the sound you hear from your partner.

What happens if my partner disconnects?

The room shows connection status and attempts to help the guest reconnect while the host keeps the room open. Online, offline, and away indicators help explain whether a player is temporarily unavailable. If the host closes the room, the session ends. Unstable networks or browser restrictions may prevent a peer-to-peer connection, so trying another network may help.

Does Truth or Dare force anyone to complete a challenge?

No. The current player chooses Truth or Dare, completes a task, and asks their partner to approve it. The partner can approve the task or request another attempt. A turn only changes following approval. Either partner should respect boundaries, and the active player can skip an uncomfortable task without losing points or receiving a penalty.

Are messages and love notes saved permanently?

No permanent chat or note storage is provided by this website. Messages and game state are held in the active browser session and are cleared when the room ends. The app uses a third-party signaling service to connect browsers, so avoid sending passwords, financial information, or other sensitive details. Only share the invitation with someone you trust.
